### Coverage Summary

- **Deep coverage (4+ lessons):** Strands 2, 4, 7, 8, 9, 17–19
- **Moderate coverage (2–3 lessons):** Strands 1, 3, 5, 6
- **Not explicitly covered:** Strands 10–16 (historical/philosophical foundations of government, some of which are addressed implicitly through primary source excerpts and case law)

### Note on Historical/Philosophical Strands (10–16)

§113.44(c)(10–16) address the philosophical foundations of American government (Enlightenment thinkers, Federalist Papers, landmark documents). These strands are less data-amenable but can be supplemented with:
- Primary source reading assignments alongside data labs
- Excerpts from the Federalist Papers when discussing separation of powers (Unit 4)
- Declaration of Independence and constitutional principles in the voting rights timeline (Unit 1)
- Teachers may wish to add 1–2 reading-focused lessons per unit to cover these strands more thoroughly
