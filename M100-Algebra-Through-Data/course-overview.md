## Course Description

M100 completes the Algebra I credit for students who took (or are taking) S400. S400 covers approximately 85% of Algebra I through economic applications — linear functions, exponential functions, scatterplots, correlation, systems of equations, and basic quadratics. M100 fills the remaining ~15%: polynomial operations, factoring trinomials, quadratic functions in depth (graphing, vertex form, transformations), all four methods of solving quadratics (factoring, square roots, completing the square, quadratic formula), radical expressions, sequences, and exponent laws. Together, S400 + M100 = 100% Algebra I §111.39 coverage.

The course is data-driven in character. Polynomial operations model real phenomena (area, volume, growth). Factoring unlocks the structure of quadratic data. Solving quadratics answers applied questions — projectile motion, revenue optimization, geometric design. Radical expressions appear in distance formulas and scientific measurement. Sequences and exponent laws formalize patterns students first encountered informally in S400. RStudio, Desmos, and GeoGebra are used throughout so that algebraic manipulation always connects to visual and computational verification.

## Connection to S400

Students entering M100 from S400 bring the following skills and concepts:

| From S400 | How M100 Builds On It |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Linear functions (`y = mx + b`) | M100 extends to polynomial and quadratic functions |
| Exponential growth (`A = P(1 + r)^t`) | M100 formalizes exponent laws and connects to sequences |
| Scatterplots and trend lines | M100 fits quadratic (not just linear) models to data |
| Correlation coefficient (`r`) | M100 uses residuals to compare linear vs. quadratic fit |
| Systems of equations (2 linear) | M100 solves systems involving quadratics |
| Basic quadratic revenue model (`R = aP - bP^2`) from S400 Unit 5 | M100 deepens: graphing, vertex form, all solving methods |
| R/RStudio fundamentals | M100 continues using R for computation and visualization |
| `ggplot2` visualization | M100 adds quadratic curve plotting and sequence visualization |

## Materials & Data Sources

- **Desmos:** Interactive graphing for polynomial, quadratic, and radical function exploration
- **GeoGebra:** Virtual algebra tiles, geometric area models for polynomial multiplication and factoring
- **RStudio:** Computational verification, data visualization, sequence generation
- **Graphing calculators:** Quadratic formula computation, table/graph exploration
- **Real-world datasets:** Projectile motion data (physics), area/volume problems (architecture), revenue modeling (economics), population sequences (biology), measurement with radicals (engineering)
