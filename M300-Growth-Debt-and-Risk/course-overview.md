## Course Description

M300 is a dual-path course that addresses a fundamental question: How does mathematics govern the financial, probabilistic, and structural systems that shape adult life? Students choose one of two paths at enrollment, and each path earns a full credit in its respective subject. Both paths share a thematic core — growth, debt, and risk — but approach it through different mathematical lenses.

**Path A (Mathematical Models with Applications §111.43)** immerses students in the mathematics of personal and civic decision-making. Personal finance, loan amortization, insurance pricing, asset depreciation, probability, and data-driven decisions are taught through real datasets, spreadsheets, and RStudio. Every formula answers a question a student will face: How much will this loan really cost? Is this insurance worth it? What does the data actually say? Path A provides 100% coverage of Math Models TEKS §111.43(c)(2–8).

**Path B (Algebra II §111.40)** extends the algebraic toolkit students built in M100 and M200. Polynomial and rational functions model complex growth and decay. Logarithmic and exponential functions formalize the compound growth students encountered informally in S400. Systems of equations and matrices provide tools for multi-variable analysis. Sequences and series connect discrete patterns to continuous models. Every concept is anchored to real-world applications — population growth, financial modeling, engineering design — so that Algebra II never feels abstract for its own sake. Path B provides 100% coverage of Algebra II TEKS §111.40(c)(2–8).

Both paths use RStudio, spreadsheets, Desmos, and financial calculators. Both paths prepare students for the S700 Capstone and for post-secondary mathematics. Students choose their path based on their goals: Path A for students heading toward business, social science, or applied fields; Path B for students heading toward STEM, engineering, or further mathematics.

## Materials & Data Sources

- **Spreadsheets (Google Sheets / Excel):** Budgeting, amortization tables, depreciation schedules, data analysis
- **RStudio:** Statistical modeling, simulation, visualization, matrix operations
- **Desmos:** Function exploration, curve fitting, probability distributions, sliders for parameter exploration
- **Financial calculators (TI BA II Plus or equivalent):** Time-value-of-money computations, loan calculations
- **Graphing calculators (TI-84 or equivalent):** Polynomial graphing, system solving, sequence generation
- **Real-world datasets:** Federal Reserve (FRED) interest rate data, BLS inflation data, insurance actuary tables, stock market historical data, Census income data, local Houston housing and vehicle data
