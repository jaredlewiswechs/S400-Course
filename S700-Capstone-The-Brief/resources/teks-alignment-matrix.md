## Statistics §111.47 — Cumulative Application (~95%)

S700 does not introduce new TEKS but provides the deepest, most independent application of all Statistics content covered in S400–S600.

### Application by Phase

| Phase | Statistics TEKS Applied |
|-------|----------------------|
| Phase 1 (Scoping) | (c)(2): Statistical process design — formulating a question, planning data collection and analysis |
| Phase 2 (Data) | (c)(2): Data collection, organization; (c)(4): Distribution assessment, normality checks |
| Phase 3 (Analysis) | (c)(4): Distributions, CLT, sampling distributions; (c)(5): Confidence intervals; (c)(6): Hypothesis testing, p-values, Type I/II; (c)(7): Regression, residuals, model selection |
| Phase 4 (Brief) | (c)(2): Communicating statistical findings; (c)(5)–(7): Reporting all inferential results |
| Phase 5 (Defense) | (c)(2)–(7): Oral explanation and defense of all statistical methods and results |
| Phase 6 (Portfolio) | Reflection on statistical growth across the full sequence |

### Cumulative TEKS Coverage Across the Sequence

| TEKS | S400 | S500 | S600 | S700 |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| (c)(2) Statistical process | Introduced | Applied | Extended | Mastered (original) |
| (c)(3) Probability | — | — | Introduced | Applied |
| (c)(4) Distributions | Introduced | Extended | Deep (normal, CLT, z) | Applied (original) |
| (c)(5) Confidence intervals | — | Introduced | Deep | Applied (original) |
| (c)(6) Hypothesis testing | — | Introduced | Deep (t, χ², ANOVA) | Applied (original) |
| (c)(7) Bivariate data | Introduced | Extended | Deep (multiple, logistic) | Applied (original) |

**Final coverage estimate:** S700 completes ~95% of Statistics §111.47. The remaining ~5% consists of specific probability counting principles (permutations/combinations) which can be supplemented if needed.
